# Extension and app, side by side: second parity pass (2026-11-16)

Branch `feature/ext-ux-parity-2`. The first pass ([EXTENSION_UX_AUDIT_2026-11-16.md](EXTENSION_UX_AUDIT_2026-11-16.md)) brought the palette, Kriti and Uljhan and the Support rename; the owner's verdict was that the extension still did not feel like the Android app. This pass takes the app's own components, assets and motion and puts them in the extension.

Source of truth: the Android app at `feature/android-3.6.2-study` (`PaperKit.kt`, `ControlsPaper.kt`, `StudyPaper.kt`, `BrandPalette.kt`, `NativeMotion.kt`, `StudyMascotView.kt`, `MascotStates.kt`, `LoadingPersonality.kt`, `DoodleKit.kt`, `UiSound.kt`, `NativeHeroBackground.kt`, and `docs/app/design-system/`).

Captures: [`screens-2026-11-16-pass2/`](screens-2026-11-16-pass2/), light and dark for every surface, 1360 px wide, Companion on Lively unless named. The app's reference screens used below are copied (downsized) into [`screens-2026-11-16-pass2/android/`](screens-2026-11-16-pass2/android/) so this page reads on its own.

## The gap list

What differed, found by putting each Android screen beside the extension surface, and what this pass did.

## Size

- Mascot art: 1.27 MB (budget 1.3 MB, test-guarded), every file the app's own and every file used.
- Crayon icons: 82 SVGs, 238 KB; player-only icons are not shipped.
- Sounds: 13 cues, 72 KB, each under the app's 8 KB limit.
- Crayon edges: 34 KB of CSS (fourteen data URIs).
- Doodles added: three files, 17 KB. Hero poster: 50 KB.

## Left for later

- The dashboard hero copy and the Google Play promotion stay; the app's hub has neither. The hub now sits under the hero, so a later pass can shorten or fold the hero.
- Kriti's thinking pose ships for "waiting for the other device" (`moment('thinking')`), but the pairing screen does not call it yet.
- The in-page shoo plays every time a blocked video opens; the app caps it at three plays a day.
- The undo bar after a removal (the app's `PaperUndoBar`) and the row-add wash and underline are not built; removals still reload the list.
- Rolling counters on the tiles (the app's `RollingCounterView`) are not built; numbers change at once.
- The keyword and channel rows themselves (chips for Comments, Exact, Date) keep the extension's own layout; only their colours and type follow the app.
- The popup's fields keep their wrapper height; the app has no popup to match exactly.
- The extension still requests Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit and two other families from Google Fonts for older styles; nothing on these pages depends on them now, so the request could go.
