The merged local catalog now has 1,672 keyed messages in each of the 38 target languages, alongside 1,135 static dashboard fragments per language and the historical release-note translations. The additional 73 managed-link policy messages were translated locally and merged from two independently owned drafts (19 and 18 non-English locales). `node scripts/check-ui-locales.mjs --require-all --require-all-static` passed at the earlier managed-policy checkpoint. This count is not whole-interface coverage. Generated Family Devices controls, remaining dashboard actions, and shared component/runtime labels are being audited and wired separately.
